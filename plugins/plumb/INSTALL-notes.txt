
--- Plumb ---
Plumb is a mono effect: put it on a vocal track (on a stereo track it corrects the sum). Set the key and the scale,
pick the voice range, and sing. The latency it adds is reported to the host: Live 0.67 ms, Tight 2.7 ms (the
default), Studio 21 ms. In Ableton Live it appears as "Audio Tools Plumb".

plumbrender runs a WAV file through Plumb with a preset:
  plumbrender in.wav out.wav --preset "Natural Lead" --set key=9 --set scale=2
  plumbrender in.wav out.wav --preset "Hard Tune" --set key=4 --set scale=1 --at 6 style=50
key is 0 to 11 from C; scale is the position in the list (0 Chromatic, 1 Major, 2 Minor, ...).
On macOS: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine plumbrender (the first time).
