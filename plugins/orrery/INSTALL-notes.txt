
--- Orrery: playing synths / tocar sintes ---
Orrery is a MIDI generator: it plays other instruments, it does not make sound by itself
(Preview sound, in Outputs, is a simple built-in synth for trying it out).
- Ableton Live: load the VST3 on a MIDI track. On each synth track set MIDI From = that track,
  "Audio Tools Orrery", and Monitor = In. Live sends AU plugins' MIDI nowhere, so use the VST3.
  Live merges channels between tracks, so to give each synth its own lane add one more Orrery
  per synth on its own track: the first Orrery is the Main, each new one becomes a satellite
  that plays the next lane, and each synth takes MIDI From its satellite's track. On macOS and
  Linux, "Publish lanes as MIDI ports" (Simple page) also gives each lane its own MIDI port.
- Preview sound (the button next to Play) is Orrery's own small synth: turn it off when your
  synths play.
- Hardware synths: in Outputs, pick the synth's USB or MIDI interface port for each lane.
- Standalone: pick the ports in Outputs; MIDI clock out is there too.

Orrery es un generador de MIDI: toca otros instrumentos (Preview sound, en Outputs, es un sinte
simple para probarlo; se apaga con el boton al lado de Play). En Ableton Live cargar el VST3 en
una pista MIDI y en cada pista de sinte elegir MIDI From = esa pista, "Audio Tools Orrery" y
Monitor = In. Live no saca el MIDI de los AU, asi que usar el VST3. Para darle a cada sinte su
carril, agregar una Orrery mas por sinte en su propia pista: la primera es la Main y cada nueva
un satelite que toca el proximo carril; cada sinte toma el MIDI de su satelite. En macOS y Linux
"Publish lanes as MIDI ports" (pagina Simple) da ademas un puerto MIDI por carril. Para sintes
fisicos, elegir en Outputs el puerto USB o la interfaz MIDI de cada carril.
