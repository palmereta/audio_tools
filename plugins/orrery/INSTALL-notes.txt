
--- Orrery: playing synths / tocar sintes ---
Orrery is a MIDI generator: it plays other instruments, it does not make sound by itself
(Preview sound, in Outputs, is a simple built-in synth for trying it out).
- Ableton Live: load the VST3 on a MIDI track. On each synth track set MIDI From = that track,
  "Orrery", and the lane's channel (Lane 1 = channel 1, and so on). Live sends AU plugins' MIDI
  nowhere, so use the VST3. Live merges channels when routing between tracks: to split lanes,
  use a virtual port in Outputs (IAC Driver on macOS, loopMIDI on Windows) or one Orrery per
  track with Link set to Lead and Follow.
- Hardware synths: in Outputs, pick the synth's USB or MIDI interface port for each lane.
- Standalone: pick the ports in Outputs; MIDI clock out is there too.

Orrery es un generador de MIDI: toca otros instrumentos (Preview sound, en Outputs, es un sinte
simple para probarlo). En Ableton Live cargar el VST3 en una pista MIDI y en cada pista de sinte
elegir MIDI From = esa pista, "Orrery" y el canal del carril. Live no saca el MIDI de los AU, asi
que usar el VST3. Para separar carriles usar un puerto virtual en Outputs (IAC Driver en macOS,
loopMIDI en Windows) o una Orrery por pista con Link en Lead y Follow. Para sintes fisicos,
elegir en Outputs el puerto USB o la interfaz MIDI de cada carril.
